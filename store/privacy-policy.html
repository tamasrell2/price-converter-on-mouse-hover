Privacy Policy — Price Converter on Mouse Hover

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.6.1 and later

In short: Price Converter on Mouse Hover does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data or browsing information. Prices are recognised and converted entirely on your device. The only network requests it makes are anonymous downloads of public exchange-rate tables.

What the extension does with web pages

To show a converted price, the extension reads the short piece of visible text around your mouse pointer on the page you are viewing and checks whether it is a price. This happens only in your browser's memory, at the moment you hover. Page content is never saved, logged, or sent anywhere. The extension does not alter the page's own content; it only overlays a small bubble with the converted price and lightly highlights the recognised price while you hover.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings — the currency you want prices shown in, and what to do with prices already in that currency. These are saved in your browser's extension settings storage. If you use Chrome sync, Google syncs these settings between your own devices as part of Chrome sync; the developer never receives them.
  • The exchange-rate table — a list of public currency rates, cached locally so it does not have to be downloaded on every page, and refreshed about every 6 hours.

Nothing else is stored. There is no account, no cookies set by the extension, no analytics, and no tracking.

Your location

To preselect a sensible currency, the extension looks at your computer's time zone and browser language setting (for example, "Europe/Berlin" suggests the euro). This check runs locally; neither your location nor the result is stored separately or sent anywhere. If it cannot tell, it uses the US dollar. You can change the currency at any time.

Network requests and third-party services

The extension downloads public exchange-rate tables from the following services. The requests contain no information about you or the pages you visit. As with any web request, these services can see your IP address; please refer to their own privacy policies.

  • ExchangeRate-API — open.er-api.com (primary source)
  • Frankfurter, European Central Bank reference rates — api.frankfurter.dev (backup)
  • jsDelivr CDN, fawazahmed0 currency-api — cdn.jsdelivr.net (second backup)

Only data (rate tables) is downloaded; no code is loaded from these services.

Data sharing and selling

No data is collected, so none is shared, sold, or transferred to anyone, and nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or credit decisions.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the extension removes its settings and cached exchange rates from your browser. You can also disable it at any time in Chrome's extension manager (chrome://extensions).

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices ever change, this page will be updated before the new version is published, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Adatvédelmi tájékoztató (magyar összefoglaló)

A Price Converter on Mouse Hover (magyarul: Ár átváltó) bővítmény semmilyen személyes adatot vagy böngészési információt nem gyűjt, nem tárol, nem ad el és nem oszt meg.

  • Az árak felismerése és átváltása teljes egészében a gépeden történik: a bővítmény csak az egérmutató körüli rövid szöveget olvassa el, a memóriában, és sehova nem küldi.
  • Tárolt adat csak a beállításaid (célvaluta, második valuta) és a letöltött árfolyamtábla. Bekapcsolt Chrome-szinkronizálásnál a beállításokat a Google szinkronizálja a saját eszközeid között; a fejlesztőhöz nem jutnak el.
  • Az alapértelmezett valutát a gép időzónájából és a böngésző nyelvéből állapítja meg, helyben; ezt sehova nem küldi.
  • Egyetlen hálózati kérése a nyilvános árfolyamtáblák névtelen letöltése (open.er-api.com, api.frankfurter.dev, cdn.jsdelivr.net).
  • A bővítmény eltávolításával minden tárolt adata törlődik.
